Privacy Policy & Support

Frosthost: Hero Siege is a relaxing single-player strategy game. Players take on the role of a squad commander, assembling an adventure-styled hero team and engaging in battles within snowy mountain valleys. It features a laid-back pace, suitable for casual short play sessions. The game works completely offline. Below you will find how your data is handled, plus answers to common questions.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1We Don't Collect Anything

Frosthost: Hero Siege runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, store, or share any personal information, usage data, photos, or game progress with us or any third party.

Support & FAQ

Everything you need to know to get started.

What can I do in Frosthost: Hero Siege?

  • Build your squad: tap UNITS at the bottom left to see your deployed and undeployed heroes, and choose who joins the fight.
  • Push through stages: tap BATTLE at the bottom right, pick a stage on the chapter map, check its TOTAL CP and star goals, then tap START.
  • Deploy mid-battle: during a fight, tap a hero card at the bottom to send that hero in; every deployment spends energy from the bar above the cards.
  • Cast abilities: tap the ability buttons along the bottom of the battlefield to turn the fight in your favour.
  • Upgrade heroes: open a hero and use UPGRADE, STAR UP or GEAR to raise health, attack and defense.
  • Earn rewards: clearing a stage grants crests, gems and gold, with extra stars for finishing at full HP.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything runs offline.

How do I start a battle?

  1. Tap BATTLE at the bottom right of the main screen.
  2. Pick a stage on the chapter map and tap START.
  3. Once the fight begins, tap your hero cards at the bottom to deploy them onto the field — deploying costs energy.
  4. Break through the enemy gate before the timer runs out; its HP bar sits at the top of the screen.

How do I upgrade my hero and grow stronger?

  1. Tap UNITS at the bottom left and pick a hero.
  2. Check the stat row: heart is health, sword is attack, shield is defense, and the number under the portrait is the hero's power.
  3. Choose UPGRADE to spend gold and crests, STAR UP to push a hero to the next tier, or GEAR to equip parts.
  4. Heroes that are not deployed cannot be upgraded — deploy them first if the upgrade button is locked.

What are crests, gold and gems for?

  • Gold (gold coin icon): the main currency for hero upgrades; the amount is shown in the top bar.
  • Gems (purple crystal icon): a rarer currency used to unlock and speed up progression.
  • Crests (blue emblem icon): upgrade material for heroes, mainly earned from stage rewards.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your hero level, gold, gems and collected parts are all saved to local storage on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. Uninstalling the game removes everything.
