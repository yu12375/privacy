Support & FAQ

Everything you need to know to get started.

What can I do in Aeonian Gloaming: Monomachy?

  • Adventurous brawls: tap Battle in the bottom right of the home screen to enter battle and defeat your foes.
  • Abilities and basic attacks: chain the ability buttons on the bottom right with the basic attack on the bottom left to break through your enemies.
  • Hero upgrades: open Hero to level up your hero, raising attack, defense and health.
  • Quests and rewards: use Quest, Achieve and Check to view quests, achievements and daily check-in rewards.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything runs offline.

How do I start a brawl?

  1. Tap Start on the title screen to reach the home screen.
  2. Use the arrows beside your hero portrait to switch the hero you take into battle.
  3. Tap Battle in the bottom right to enter battle.
  4. Score more points within the time limit to win the round, or tap the back arrow in the top left to return home.

How do I upgrade my hero and grow stronger?

  1. Tap Hero at the bottom of the home screen.
  2. Check your hero's level in the middle (e.g. LV.3) and the stat panel on the right: red is attack, blue is defense, green is health.
  3. Tap Upgrade and spend gold; all stats rise with each level.

What are gold and gems for?

  • Gold (gold circular icon): the main currency for hero upgrades and part upgrades; the amount is shown in the top right of the home screen and the Hero screen.
  • Gems (purple crystal icon): a rarer resource used to unlock and speed up progression.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your hero level, gold, gems and collected parts are all saved to local storage on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. Uninstalling the game removes everything.

支持与常见问题

开始游戏前你可能会想了解的内容。

在 Aeonian Gloaming: Monomachy 里可以做什么？

  • 冒险格斗：点击首页右下角的 Battle 进入战斗，击败你的敌人。
  • 技能与普攻：将右下角的技能按钮与左下角的普通攻击串联，突破敌阵。
  • 英雄升级：打开 Hero 提升英雄等级，提高攻击、防御与生命。
  • 任务与奖励：通过 Quest、Achieve 和 Check 查看任务、成就与每日签到奖励。

需要联网吗？

不需要，全部离线运行。

如何开始一场战斗？

  1. 在标题界面点击 Start 进入首页。
  2. 使用英雄头像旁的箭头切换出战英雄。
  3. 点击右下角的 Battle 进入战斗。
  4. 在限定时间内取得更高分数即可赢得该回合；或点击左上角的返回箭头回到首页。

如何升级英雄、变得更强？

  1. 点击首页底部的 Hero。
  2. 查看中间显示的英雄等级（如 LV.3）以及右侧的属性面板：红色为攻击，蓝色为防御，绿色为生命。
  3. 点击 Upgrade 并消耗金币，每提升一级所有属性都会增长。

金币和宝石有什么用途？

  • 金币（金色圆形图标）：用于英雄升级与部件升级的主要货币；数量显示在首页与 Hero 界面的右上角。
  • 宝石（紫色水晶图标）：更稀有的资源，用于解锁并加速成长进度。

我的进度会保存吗？

会。你的英雄等级、金币、宝石与已收集的部件都会保存在设备的本地存储中，因此你可以随时继续上次的进度。 卸载游戏将移除全部数据。
